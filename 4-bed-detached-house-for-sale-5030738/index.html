Exceptional Extended Four-Bedroom Family Home with Impressive 2021 Contemporary Extension and South-Facing Rear Garden

DNG Kelly Duncan is delighted to present to the market for sale No. 11 Tegan Court, Mucklagh, Tullamore, Co. Offaly, an exceptional extended four-bedroom family home enjoying an enviable position overlooking a large green area within this established and highly regarded residential development.

Originally constructed in 2002 and extending to an impressive 195 m² / 2,099 ft², No. 11 has been beautifully maintained and significantly enhanced by its current owners, most notably through the addition of an impressive contemporary extension completed in 2021. The result is a substantial and highly versatile family home combining generous proportions with exceptional natural light, four genuine double bedrooms, two reception rooms and superb connectivity with its south-facing rear garden.

The 2021 extension forms a defining feature of the property. Designed around light and connection with the garden, it incorporates large picture windows, multiple roof lights and full-height glazed doors opening directly onto the patio and gardens. A dropped pelmet ceiling with feature lighting and electrically operated blinds further enhance this impressive contemporary space.

At ground-floor level, an entrance porch opens to a welcoming hallway with bespoke understairs storage and cloakroom. The sitting room provides a comfortable separate reception space centred around a contemporary freestanding solid-fuel stove, while the extended kitchen and dining area forms the true heart of the home.

Bathed in natural light, the kitchen is extensively fitted with floor and eye-level cabinetry complemented by quartz countertops and matching upstands. A substantial breakfast counter with waterfall quartz edge provides additional preparation and informal dining space, while a Quooker tap, fitted water softener, bespoke window seating with integrated storage and carefully considered feature lighting add to the specification.

Immediately adjoining the kitchen/dining area is a second living room, finished with solid timber flooring and centred around an inset solid-fuel stove with granite surround and tiled hearth. Bespoke fitted fireside cabinetry and bookshelving complete this comfortable additional family living space.

A generously fitted utility room provides raised provision for laundry appliances, space for an American-style fridge/freezer, extensive storage and direct rear access, with a guest toilet completing the ground-floor accommodation.

At first-floor level, the upgraded staircase and landing make an immediate impression. The newly fitted staircase, installed by Dunnes of Rahugh, incorporates a contemporary glazed balustrade with contrasting oak handrail and cappings. There are four generous double bedrooms, including a rear-aspect principal bedroom with built-in wardrobes and private en-suite, together with a contemporary family bathroom. A fully shelved hot press provides further storage, while pull-down access leads to a partially floored attic.

The property carries a BER B rating, reflecting its strong energy efficiency and providing the added benefit of eligibility for Green Mortgage products, subject to individual lender criteria. The home has also benefited from recent investment in its heating system, with both the oil tank and oil burner recently replaced. Oil-fired central heating incorporates zoned controls and can be conveniently controlled remotely via Wi-Fi.

Externally, the standard of presentation continues. To the front, a generous tarmacadam driveway provides ample off-street parking, bordered by lawn and beautifully established landscaped beds with mature shrubs, trees and colourful planting. A rendered walled boundary with entrance gate piers completes the approach.

The south-facing rear garden has been thoughtfully arranged for both family life and outdoor entertaining. A paved patio extends from the contemporary extension, beyond which lies lawn, raised flower beds and established planting. A covered pergola creates an excellent sheltered outdoor dining and entertaining area, while a steel garden shed provides useful additional storage.

Mucklagh offers a highly convenient setting, combining the appeal and community atmosphere of village living with Tullamore town immediately on its doorstep.

The village is well served by local amenities and community facilities, including St. Colman's National School, Shamrocks GAA Club and Mucklagh Community Centre, together with everyday services and recreational amenities. Tullamore is only a short drive away, providing an extensive range of shopping, schools, restaurants, sporting facilities, professional services and transport connections.

Extending to approximately 195 m² / 2,099 ft², with four double bedrooms, two reception rooms, an impressive contemporary extension and a beautifully presented south-facing rear garden, No. 11 Tegan Court offers an exceptional combination of space, quality and versatility in a highly convenient village setting on the doorstep of Tullamore.
